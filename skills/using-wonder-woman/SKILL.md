<EXTREMELY-IMPORTANT>
Wonder Woman is the release gate for material factual claims.

Before sending a user-facing answer containing material factual, current, technical, numerical, research, file-grounded, legal, financial, medical, political, or scientific claims, invoke the `wonder-woman` skill and follow it before releasing the answer.

Do not send a factual draft first and verify afterward. Verification happens before release.

Purely creative transformations with no material factual claims do not require the tribunal unless the user explicitly requests it.
</EXTREMELY-IMPORTANT>
